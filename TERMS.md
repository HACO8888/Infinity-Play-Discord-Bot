## 服務內容 / The service

本機器人提供自動管理（偵測外部邀請連結、詐騙／釣魚網址、詐騙訊息、洗版、冒充管理員、可疑的自訂狀態與突襲）、管理紀錄與一般資訊指令。偵測結果僅供管理員參考；除外部邀請連結與已知詐騙網址會自動刪除外，是否處置由伺服器管理員決定。

The bot provides automated moderation (external invites, scam/phishing links, scam messages, flooding, staff impersonation, suspicious custom statuses and raids), a moderation log and informational commands. Detections are advisory; apart from external invites and known scam links being removed automatically, server moderators decide what action to take.
